# Tirana roadside trees

The broadleaf set uses three original silhouettes inspired by plane, linden
and acacia. Each has tapered trunks, continuous forked branches, and shaded
leaf sprays arranged around the forks in depth. Near and middle trees share
branch positions, crown proportions and bark. A four-azimuth impostor bake
uses the matching middle mesh for each species. Tirana keeps its mapped tree
positions and the separate pine replacements.

| Tier | Faces per tree | Surfaces | Geometry |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Previous detailed tree | 2,273 | 3 | 2,049 bark and branch triangles plus 224 card triangles |
| LOD0 | 566 | 2 | 384 bark triangles with roots and secondary forks, plus 182 spray triangles |
| LOD1 | 171 | 2 | 93 trunk and major-branch triangles, plus 78 larger spray triangles |
| LOD2 | 2 | 1 | One cylindrical billboard with four baked views per species |
| Previous billboard | 4 | 1 | Two crossed photographic cards |

Both 3D tiers use the same bark and foliage materials across all three
species. The bark normal map follows the tapered geometry. Foliage uses
two-sided alpha scissor and rounded vertex normals, with a small backlight
term.

All four textures use compressed mip chains. The foliage shader lowers the
coverage cutoff as the alpha-tested sprays recede. The far bake uses padded
tiles and a lower cutoff for shrinking silhouettes. Existing
`photographic_tree.png` is 1280 × 2000, mipmapped and VRAM compressed. Its
PNG All attribution and CC BY-SA 4.0 licence are recorded in

and branch set is Poly Haven Tree Small 02 by Rico Cilliers and Rob Tuytel,
CC0 1.0. The new bark maps are resized from it. The new geometry, spray
atlas and impostors are generated by the repository's Python baker.

## Runtime settings

| Preset | Nominal LOD0 range | Nominal LOD1 outer range | LOD0 / LOD1 settled pool caps | Leaf wind amplitude |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Low | 28 m | 76 m | 9 / 45 | 0 cm |
| Medium | 50 m | 125 m | 20 / 92 | 3.3 cm |
| High | 70 m | 160 m | 30 / 144 | 5.8 cm |

Six reusable MultiMesh pools cover the 3D tiers. The distant impostors
are grouped into 320 m cells. A 76 m search grid limits candidate selection
to cells near the camera. The camera generally rescans after 76 ms, or
after at least 34 ms when it has moved 5.5 m. Still views skip selection.
A camera cut settles tiers immediately. At normal racing speeds, tier
changes use 180 ms of complementary screen-door dither, with 5/8 m of
distance hysteresis. A refresh changes up to 24 assignments. The separate
transition budget allows up to 24 extra slots per tier while models overlap.
Dense views can reach a pool cap before the nominal radius.

Wind moves the leaves and their textured stems while the solid trunk and
branches stay fixed. It varies by deterministic tree seed and falls off
from 35 to 112 m. Adaptive pressure shrinks distances and pools, reduces
or disables wind, and drops detailed tree shadows. Unpressured High casts
near-tier tree shadows. The 3D foliage batches stay outside reflection
probe redraws, and middle and far trees retain their baked crown shading.

The bake's four orthographic azimuths per species put the LOD0 median,
p95 and maximum alpha-passing overlap at 3, 13 and 24 layers per occupied
pixel before depth rejection. LOD1 measured 3, 8 and 12 layers. About 41%
of projected card fragments pass the cutoff in both tiers. Dense crown
joins viewed through other trees are the main overdraw risk. The test is
a projected overlap estimate. Device GPU counters are still needed for
actual fragment cost and tile occupancy.

```sh
python3 tools/pipeline/build_tirana_trees.py --coverage
godot --headless --editor --import --path godot
godot --path godot --rendering-method mobile res://tests/tree_visual.tscn \
  -- env=day quality=high tag=after detail blend
```

Use the capture scene with base revision `19f2f04` for the original trees.
Use `quality=low`, `quality=medium`, `env=sunset` or `env=night` for
the other dense views, omitting `detail blend` for a single screenshot.
The scene writes PNGs to `user://tree-shots` and prints timing and
vegetation counts.

## Regression checks

All 53 launches in `tools/run_tests.py` passed with Godot 4.4.1. This
includes all six city worlds, the Mobile and Forward+ renderer checks,
and the dedicated vegetation test. The vegetation test covers the
transition lifetime, bounded pool changes, camera cuts and fast travel,
pine replacement slots, preset pressure, three species and shared
materials. All five baked assets reproduced byte-for-byte.

**60 FPS remains a physical Android target.** Sustained laps on target
handsets still need GPU counters and p95 frame pacing at all three
presets, including the rear mirror, reflection probes, combat, weather,
and thermal load. Tilted foliage views and long lines of trees should
also be checked as the camera drives across both transitions.
